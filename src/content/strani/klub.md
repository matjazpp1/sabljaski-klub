  ## Our story

  Ljubljana Fencing Club brings épée fencing to children, young people and adults in Ljubljana. In autumn 2013 we opened our own fencing hall at Tržaška 132 – the official opening took place on 11 October 2013, with a fencing bout, an exhibition of épées and photographs, and many friends of the club.

  On 28 January 2014 the club was admitted to the Slovenian Fencing Federation, the umbrella organisation for fencing in Slovenia, which is a member of the Olympic Committee of Slovenia, the International Fencing Federation (FIE) and the European Fencing Confederation (EFC).

  ## Tournaments and the club league

  In April 2014 we organised the 1st Ljubljana Tournament together with SD Gladio, and the tournament has since become a tradition – the 4th edition in 2017 counted towards the Slovenian national ranking. In the 2017/2018 season we launched a club league: a series of training competitions in which young fencers from several Slovenian clubs gain their first competition experience. Results and photos are in the [archive](/en/archive/).

  ## Our competitors

  Our members compete at national championships and international tournaments, all the way to European Cadet and Junior Championships. Our coaches also work as international FIE referees.

  ## Who we train

  - a beginners' fencing school for children,
  - recreational fencing for adults,
  - training for competitors.

  New members are welcome all year round – see [Start fencing](/en/training/start-fencing/).

## Naša zgodba

Sabljaški klub Ljubljana otrokom, mladini in odraslim v Ljubljani približuje sabljanje z mečem. Jeseni 2013 smo na Tržaški 132 odprli svojo sabljaško dvorano – uradna otvoritev je bila 11. oktobra 2013 s sabljaško borbo, razstavo mečev in fotografij ter številnimi prijatelji kluba.

28. januarja 2014 je bil klub sprejet v Sabljaško zvezo Slovenije, krovno organizacijo slovenskega sabljanja, ki je članica Olimpijskega komiteja Slovenije, Mednarodne sabljaške zveze (FIE) in Evropske sabljaške zveze (EFC).

## Turnirji in klubska liga

Aprila 2014 smo skupaj s SD Gladio organizirali 1. turnir Ljubljane, ki je nato postal tradicionalen – 4. turnir leta 2017 je štel za slovensko rang lestvico. V sezoni 2017/2018 smo začeli s klubsko ligo: nizom trening-tekmovanj, na katerih mladi sabljači iz več slovenskih klubov nabirajo prve tekmovalne izkušnje. Rezultate in fotografije najdete v [arhivu](/arhiv/).

## Naši tekmovalci

Naši člani nastopajo na državnih prvenstvih in mednarodnih turnirjih vse do evropskih prvenstev za kadete in mladince. Naša trenerja delujeta tudi kot mednarodna sodnika FIE.

## Koga treniramo

- začetna šola sabljanja za otroke,
- rekreacija za odrasle,
- treningi za tekmovalce.

Nove člane vpisujemo celo leto – več na strani [Začni s sabljanjem](/treningi/zacni-s-sabljanjem/).
