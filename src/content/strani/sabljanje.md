  ## History

  Fencing developed into a modern Olympic sport at the end of the 19th and the beginning of the 20th century. Modern sport fencing has three weapons: épée, foil and sabre. The main differences between them are their weight, how hits are scored and the valid target area.

  Fencing is one of only four sports that have featured at every modern Olympic Games, since the very first ones in Athens in 1896.

  The International Fencing Federation (FIE) was founded in Paris in 1913. In addition to the Olympic Games every four years, the best fencers from all countries compete every year at world and continental championships and World Cup events in all three weapons and in different age categories (cadets, juniors, seniors).

  ## Rudolf Cvetko

  Rudolf Cvetko was a Slovenian officer and fencer and the first Slovenian to win an Olympic medal. At the 1912 Olympic Games in Stockholm, as a member of the Austrian fencing team, he won a silver medal in the team sabre event.

  He later worked as a fencing teacher and coach in Ljubljana, in the fencing section of the Ilirija sports club. He was also a fencing referee and held senior positions in the Yugoslav Fencing Federation. After 1945 he devoted himself mainly to training young fencers at the Tabor sports club and to his work in the Slovenian Fencing Federation.

  He also organised and led fencing instructor courses, laying the foundations of Slovenian fencing. He taught fencing at the Academy of Theatre (today AGRFT) and at the Institute of Physical Education in Ljubljana (today the Faculty of Sport). For his work he received numerous national and sporting awards, including the Bloudek Award.

  ## Is it dangerous?

  No. Fencing is one of the safer sports. Épées (as well as sabres and foils) are not sharp, and fencers wear protective equipment – a mask that protects the face and head, and a special protective suit that withstands a force of over 800 newtons.

## Zgodovina

Sabljanje se je konec 19. in v začetku 20. stoletja razvilo v moderen olimpijski šport. Pri modernem športnem sabljanju poznamo tri orožja: meč, floret in sabljo. Bistvene razlike med njimi so v teži, tehniki zadetka in zadetni površini.

Sabljanje je eden izmed štirih športov, ki so prisotni na vseh olimpijskih igrah moderne dobe, že od prvih leta 1896 v Atenah.

Mednarodna sabljaška zveza (FIE) je bila ustanovljena leta 1913 v Parizu. Poleg olimpijskih iger, ki potekajo vsake štiri leta, najboljši sabljači iz vseh držav vsako leto tekmujejo še na svetovnih in celinskih prvenstvih ter tekmah svetovnega pokala v vseh treh orožjih in različnih starostnih kategorijah (kadeti, mladinci, člani).

## Rudolf Cvetko

Rudolf Cvetko je bil slovenski častnik in sabljač ter prvi Slovenec, ki je osvojil olimpijsko medaljo. Na olimpijskih igrah leta 1912 v Stockholmu je kot član avstrijske sabljaške reprezentance osvojil srebrno medaljo v ekipnem tekmovanju s sabljo.

Kasneje je deloval kot sabljaški učitelj in trener v Ljubljani, v sabljaški sekciji Športnega kluba Ilirija. Bil je tudi sabljaški sodnik in je opravljal visoke funkcije v Jugoslovanski sabljaški zvezi. Po letu 1945 se je posvetil predvsem vzgoji mladih sabljačev v Športnem klubu Tabor in delu v Sabljaški zvezi Slovenije.

Organiziral in vodil je tudi sabljaške inštruktorske tečaje in s tem postavil temelje slovenskemu sabljanju. Sabljanje je predaval na Akademiji za igralsko umetnost (danes AGRFT) in na Inštitutu za telesno vzgojo v Ljubljani (danes Fakulteta za šport). Za svoje delo je prejel vrsto državnih in društvenih priznanj, med drugim tudi Bloudkovo nagrado.

## Je nevarno?

Ne. Sabljanje je eden izmed bolj varnih športov. Meči (pa tudi sablje in floreti) niso ostri, sabljači pa nosimo zaščitno opremo – masko, ki zaščiti obraz in glavo, ter posebno zaščitno obleko, ki vzdrži silo nad 800 newtonov.
