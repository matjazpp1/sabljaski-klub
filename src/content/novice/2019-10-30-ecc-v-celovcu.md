  On Saturday, 26 October 2019, a [European Cadet Circuit competition took place in Klagenfurt](https://www.eurofencing.info/competitions/latest-results/case:competitions/tournamentId:666).  
  Eva Figelj competed among the cadet women. Unfortunately she didn't win a bout in her pool and finished 177th.

  Among the boys, as many as 5 of our fencers competed in a field of 213 épée fencers!  
  Jakob Pavlin and Viktor Štrumbelj did best, each winning 3 bouts in their pools! Unfortunately, both lost their first elimination bout. Viktor finished 133rd and Jakob 139th.  
  Lars Ponikvar won two bouts in his pool, which unfortunately wasn't enough – he was just below the cut-off line separating those who continue from those who don't – and he finished 165th.  
  Brin Božič won one bout in his pool. Unfortunately that wasn't enough to continue, and he finished 189th.  
  Bernard Baler didn't manage a win in his pool and finished 202nd.  
  The next day, Lars, Brin and Viktor also competed in the team event, which drew as many as 36 teams! They had a bye in the first elimination round and then fenced against Austria's first team, losing 45:29. They finished 25th.  
  Our coaches Eva and Gaja also refereed at the competition.

V soboto 26.10.2019 je v [Celovcu potekala tekma v evropskem kadetskem pokalu](https://www.eurofencing.info/competitions/latest-results/case:competitions/tournamentId:666).  
V kategoriji kadetinje je nastopila Eva Figelj. Žal v skupini ni dosegle zmage. Tekmo zaključila na 177. mestu.

Med fanti je v konkurenci 213 mečistov nastopilo kar 5 naših fantov!  
Najbolje sta se odrezala Jakob Pavlin in Viktor Štrumbelj, ki sta v skupini dosegla kar 3 zmage! Žal sta oba prvo eliminacijsko borbo izgubila. Viktor je na koncu zasedel 133. mesto, Jakob pa 139. mesto.  
Lars Ponikvar je v skupini dosegel dve zmagi, kar pa na žalost ni bilo dovolj, saj je bil tik pod črto, ki ločuje tekmovalce ki nadaljujejo tekmovanje in tiste, ki ne. S tem je zaključil na 165. mestu.  
Brin Božič je v skupini dosegel eno zmago. Žal to ni bilo dovolj za nadaljevanje tekmovanja, ter se je uvrstil na 189. mesto  
Bernard Baler v skupini ni uspel doseči zmage, tako se je uvrstil na 202. mesto.  
Naslednji dan so Lars, Brin in Viktor nastopili tudi na ekipnem delu tekmovanja. Nastopilo je kar 36 ekip! Prvemu krogu eliminacij so se izognili, nato pa so sabljali proti prvi ekipi Avstrije in izgubili z rezultatom 45:29. Uvrstili so se na 25. mesto.  
Na tekmovanju sta sodili tudi naši trenerki, Eva in Gaja.
