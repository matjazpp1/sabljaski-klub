  On Saturday, 19 January 2019, the national cadet, junior and student fencing championships took place in Velenje. 9 of our fencers took part, with 13 entries in total.  
  Lars, Brin, Viktor and Bernard competed in cadet men's épée. Viktor did best, fencing very well and finishing 3rd! Bernard made the top 8, and Lars and Brin also fenced good bouts and gained experience in the older category, which they will move up to next season.

  Later, Eva also competed among the junior women and improved on that result, finishing an excellent 3rd!  
  Bernard, Viktor and Dominik competed in junior men's épée. Bernard and Viktor made the top 8, and Dominik finished 9th.  
  Dominik then also competed in junior men's sabre. It was his first time fencing sabre, yet he still won a bout in his pool!  
  Miha competed among the student men and finished 6th.  
  Eva competed among the student women and finished 1st.  
  We are very proud of all our fencers – they fenced well and, above all, have made great progress over the past year! :)

V soboto, 19.1.2019 je v Velenju potekalo kadetsko, mladinsko in študentsko državno prvenstvo v sabljanju. Udeležilo se ga je 9 naših sabljačev, ki so skupaj zbrali 13 nastopov.  
V kategoriji meč kadeti so nastopili Lars, Brin, Viktor in Bernard. Najbolje je šlo Viktorju, ki je sabljal zelo dobro in dosegel 3. mesto! Bernard se je uvrstil med najboljših 8, Lars in Brin pa sta prav tako prikazala dobre borbe in nabirala izkušnje v starejši kategoriji, v katero bosta prestopila naslednjo sezono.  
V kategoriji meč kadetinje sta sabljali Eva in Nika. Za Niko je bila to sploh prva tekma, žal se ni uvrstila čez skupine, je pa dobila dragocene izkušnje za naslednja tekmovanja. Eva se je uvrstila med najboljših 8.  
Kasneje je Eva nastopila še med mladinkami in nadgradila rezultat ter dosegla odlično 3. mesto!  
V kategoriji meč mladinci so se pomerili Bernard, Viktor in Dominik. Bernard in Viktor sta se uvrstila med 8, Dominik pa je zasedel 9. mesto.  
Dominik se je nato pomeril še med mladinci v sablji. Prvič je sabljal sabljo, vendar je kljub temu dosegel zmago v skupini!  
Med študenti je nastopil Miha in zasedel 6. mesto.  
Med študentkami pa je nastopila Eva in zasedla 1. mesto.  
Na vse naše sabljače smo zelo ponosni, saj so dobro sabljali in predvsem zelo napredovali v zadnjem letu! :)  
Nekaj utrinkov s tekmovanja pa lahko pogledate na fotografijah.
