  The individual cadet events at the European Cadet and Junior Championships in Naples, Italy, are over. Both our members, Jurij Beber and Vito Hvala, fenced superbly. In his pool Jurij beat fencers from Azerbaijan and Bulgaria and, with two wins and four defeats, placed 94th, reaching the elimination rounds at his very first European Championships.

  After a narrow opening defeat to a Norwegian fencer, Vito went on to beat opponents from Croatia, Portugal and Austria, as well as the eventual finalist from Great Britain. Despite a loss to a Dutch fencer, he won his pool with four wins and two defeats and was ranked a very good 34th after the qualifying bouts.

  Unfortunately, our two fencers then met each other in the first qualifying bout for a place in the last 64.

  Vito won the club duel, after which he faced a tough opponent, Ukraine's Maksym Gula, the fourth seed. After a tense bout with as many as seven double hits, Vito lost 10:15. He finished the European Championships in an excellent 42nd place out of 127 fencers – one of the best Slovenian results at the European Cadet and Junior Championships in recent years!

Na evropskem prvenstvu za kadete in mladince v italijanskem Neaplju so zaključene individualne kadetske borbe. Oba naša člana, Jurij Beber in Vito Hvala, sta se odlično borila. Jurij je v skupini premagal predstavnika Azerbajdžana in Bolgarije ter z dvema zmagama in štirimi porazi dosegel 94. mesto, s čimer se je v eliminacijske borbe uvrstil že na svojem prvem nastopu na evropskem prvenstvu.

Vito je po začetnem tesnem porazu v skupini proti Norvežanu nadaljeval z zmagami proti Hrvatu, Portugalcu, Avstrijcu in tudi končnemu finalistu iz Velike Britanije. Kljub porazu proti Nizozemcu je s štirimi zmagami in dvema porazoma zmagal v skupini ter po kvalifikacijskih borbah zasedel zelo dobro 34. mesto.

Žal sta se oba naša predstavnika nato ravno med seboj pomerila v prvi kvalifikacijski borbi za uvrstitev med 64 najboljših.

V klubski borbi je bil Vito močnejši, po zmagi pa ga je čakal težak nasprotnik, Ukrajinec Maksym Gula, četrti nosilec prvenstva. Po napeti borbi in kar 7 dvojnih zadetkih je Vito z rezultatom 10:15 moral priznati premoč Ukrajincu. S tem je evropsko prvenstvo zaključil na odličnem 42. mestu med skupaj 127 udeleženci, kar je ena najboljših slovenskih uvrstitev na evropskih prvenstvih za kadete in mladince v zadnjih letih!

Vsi rezultati, meč kadeti: [fencingtimelive.com](https://www.fencingtimelive.com/events/results/6FBFC35E666A4970B929E4F9F7BD986A)

Čestitke Vitu in Juriju za odlične dosežke!

V začetku naslednjega tedna pa držimo pesti za Niko in Leona, ki se bosta na piste podala v mladinski kategoriji.
