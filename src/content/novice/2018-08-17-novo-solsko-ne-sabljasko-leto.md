  August is nearly over, and the coaches of Ljubljana Fencing Club have already drawn up a plan for the season starting in September!  
  Before the season starts (on Monday, 19 August), our keenest competitors will head to Pokljuka for 7 days. During this time we'll prepare them as well as we can for the coming season – they'll have fitness and fencing training and, if they behave, some free time to have fun.

  Since we opened Ljubljana Fencing Club, the number of members has grown steadily – so much so that this year we'll have TWO new groups.  
  Training starts on Monday, 3 September 2018 – please bring a completed [registration form](/dokumenti/novice/vpisni-list-2018-19.docx) (in Slovenian) to training!

  The BEGINNERS group (Tuesday 4:45–6 pm and Friday 5–6:15 pm) is for all children aged 7 to 14 who are interested in the sport.  
  This year we've also decided to offer fencing to younger children – we're introducing a FENCING NURSERY for children aged 4 to 7 (Fridays at 4 pm).  
  All our "old" fencers will mostly train on the same timetable – given how much they've learned in recent years, we'll no longer group them only by experience but also by age: the YOUNGER COMPETITORS (roughly 9–13 years) will train on Mondays, Wednesdays and Thursdays from 4:45 to 6 pm,

  and the OLDER COMPETITORS (roughly 13–18 years), as last year, on Mondays, Wednesdays and Thursdays from 6 to 7:30 pm.  
  And of course we haven't forgotten the adults – all adults, regardless of previous knowledge and experience, are welcome every Monday and Wednesday from 8 to 9:30 pm.

Avgusta bo vsak trenutek konec, trenerji Sabljaškega kluba Ljubljana pa že imamo izdelan načrt za sezono, ki se začne septembra!  
Pred začetkom sezone (že v ponedeljek, 19.8.) se bodo naši najbolj zagreti tekmovalci odpravili na Pokljuko, kjer bodo preživeli 7 dni. V tem času jih bomo kar najbolje pripravili na prihajajočo sezono - imeli bodo kondicijske treninge, sabljaške treninge, vmes pa, če bodo pridni, tudi nekaj prostega časa, da se malo pozabavajo.

Odkar smo odprli Sabljaški klub Ljubljana, je število članov vztrajno naraščalo, do te točke, da bomo letos imeli kar DVE novi skupini.  
S treningi bomo začeli v ponedeljek, 3.9.2018, na trening pa prosim prinesite izpolnjen [vpisni list](/dokumenti/novice/vpisni-list-2018-19.docx)!

Skupina ZAČETNIKOV (torek 16:45 - 18:00 in petek 17:00 - 18:15) bo namenjena vsem otrokom starim med 7 in 14 let, ki jih zanima ta šport.  
Poleg tega smo se letos odločili, da bomo sabljanje omogočili tudi mlajšim otrokom - uvedli bomo SABLJAŠKI VRTEC, ki bo namenjen otrokom med 4. in 7. letom (petki ob 16:00).  
Vsi "stari" sabljači, pa bodo imeli treninge, večinoma, po istem urniku - glede na to, koliko so se naučili v zadnjih letih, jih ne bomo več ločevali le po izkušnjah, pač pa tudi po njihovi starosti: MLAJŠI TEKMOVALCI (približno 9 - 13 let) bodo imeli treninge ob ponedeljkih, sredah in četrtkih od 16:45 do 18:00,

STAREJŠI TEKMOVALCI (približno 13 - 18 let) pa, tako kot lani, ob ponedeljkih, sredah in četrtkih od 18:00 do 19:30.  
Seveda pa nismo pozabili na odrasle - vsi odrasli, ne glede na (pred)znanje in izkušnje so dobrodošli vsak ponedeljek in sredo od 20:00 do 21:30.
