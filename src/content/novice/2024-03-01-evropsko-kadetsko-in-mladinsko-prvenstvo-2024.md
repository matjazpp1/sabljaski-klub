  This year's European Cadet and Junior Championships in Naples are over, and the last members of the Slovenian team from our club have returned home. Today the individual events for junior women's épée and junior men's sabre and foil took place. The championships close with the junior team events, in which Slovenia unfortunately does not compete.

  Nika Korošec, the last of the SK Ljubljana representatives to fence, finished her first European Junior Championships without a win in one of the toughest pools. She fought determined bouts against Europe's best épée fencers, including the top seed, who went on to win the gold medal. Nika placed 93rd. Leon Svit Černjak took to the piste on Monday and, after close bouts against the European elite, had to admit defeat. With one win in his pool he finished the European Junior Championships in 90th place.

  We congratulate our members Nika, Leon, Jurij and Vito, as well as all the other Slovenian competitors at the championships, on their wholehearted fencing. We wish them success in their next competitions and the motivation for hard work in training, which will surely bring new results.

  Special congratulations to Vito, the only Slovenian competitor to make it through both the pool and the first elimination rounds. His 42nd place in cadet men's épée was the best Slovenian result at this year's European Cadet and Junior Championships.

Letošnje kadetsko in mladinsko evropsko prvenstvo v Neaplju je za nami in še zadnji člani slovenske reprezentance iz našega kluba so se vrnili v domovino. Danes so se v individualnih borbah pomerile še mladinke v meču in mladinci v sablji in floretu. Prvenstvo se zaključuje z ekipnimi tekmovanji mladink in mladincev, kjer pa žal Slovenija ne nastopa.

Nika Korošec je kot zadnja izmed predstavnikov SK Ljubljana svoj prvi nastop na evropskem mladinskem prvenstvu zaključila brez zmage v sicer eni najtežjih skupin. Pokazala je odločne borbe proti najboljšim mečevalkam Evrope, v svoji skupini se je pomerila tudi z nosilko turnirja, ki je na koncu osvojila zlato medaljo. Nika je zasedla 93. mesto. Leon Svit Černjak se je na piste podal že v ponedeljek in po tesnih izidih v borbah z evropsko elito moral priznati premoč konkurenci. Z eno zmago v skupini je evropsko mladinsko prvenstvo zaključil na 90. mestu.

Našim članom Niki, Leonu, Juriju in Vitu ter tudi vsem drugim slovenskim udeleženkam in udeležencem prvenstva stare celine čestitamo za srčne borbe in jim želimo uspehov na prihodnjih tekmah, pa tudi motivacije za trdo delo na treningih, ki bo zagotovo prineslo nove uspehe.

Posebne čestitke pa Vitu, ki se je kot edini od vseh slovenskih udeležencev prebil tako čez skupino kot čez prve eliminacije ter z 42. mestom v kategoriji meč kadeti dosegel najboljšo slovensko uvrstitev na letošnjem evropskem kadetskem in mladinskem prvenstvu.
