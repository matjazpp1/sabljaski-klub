  On Saturday and Sunday, 9 and 10 May 2015, the national fencing championships were held at Tabor in Ljubljana – épée on Saturday, foil and sabre on Sunday. A total of 14 of our fencers took part. Our youngest fencers were the most numerous, with as many as 5 competing in the boys and girls D category. They all fenced excellent bouts and showed great progress. Brin did best of all and became national champion, while Jon, Lars, Jure and Tisa placed between 5th and 8th. Jaša, Bernard and Filip-Lan competed among the boys C: Jaša did best in 5th place, Filip-Lan was 9th and Bernard 13th. Among the girls C, Lucija took 3rd place, and she was also 3rd among the girls B. In the boys B category, Jakob was 3rd; he also competed among the cadets, finishing 9th. Dominik also competed among the cadets and placed 8th. Gregor competed among the senior men and finished 17th, while in the veterans' category he won a bronze medal. Eva and Gaja competed among the senior women: Eva became national champion and Gaja won a bronze medal.  
  They also competed on Sunday, trying their hand at sabre and foil. Eva became national champion in sabre as well and won a silver medal in foil, while Gaja won bronze in foil and sabre, just as in épée.  
  Congratulations to all our fencers!

V soboto in nedeljo, 9. in 10. maja 2015 je v Ljubljani na Taboru potekalo državno prvenstvo v sabljanju. V soboto je potekalo tekmovanje v meču, v nedeljo pa v floretu in sablji. Prvenstva se je skupaj udeležilo kar 14 naših sabljačev. Najbolj številčni so bili naši najmlajši sabljači, saj jih je v kategoriji dečki in deklice D nastopilo kar 5. Vsi so pokazali odlične borbe in velik napredek. Najbolje od vseh se je odrezal Brin, ki je postal državni prvak. Jon, Lars, Jure in Tisa pa so se zvrstili od 5. do 8. mesta. Med dečki C so nastopili Jaša, Bernard in Filip-Lan. Najbolje se je uvrstil Jaša, ki je zasedel 5. mesto, Filip-Lan je bil 9., Bernard pa je zasedel 13. mesto. Pri deklicah C pa je Lucija zasedla 3. mesto, prav tako je bila 3. pri deklicah B. V kategoriji dečki B je bil Jakob 3., pomeril pa se je še v kategoriji kadetov, kjer je bil 9. Pri kadetih je nastopil tudi Dominik in se uvrstil na 8. mesto. Pri članih je nastopil Gregor in bil 17., medtem ko je v kategoriji veteranov osvojil bronasto medaljo. Pri članicah pa sta nastopili Eva in Gaja. Eva je postala državna prvakinja, Gaja pa osvojila bronasto medaljo.  
Nastopili sta tudi v nedeljo in se preizkusili v sablji in floretu. Eva je postala državna prvakinja tudi v sablji, v floretu pa je osvojila srebrno medaljo. Gaja pa je tako kot v meču tudi v floretu in sablji osvojila bron.  
Vsem sabljačem čestitamo!
