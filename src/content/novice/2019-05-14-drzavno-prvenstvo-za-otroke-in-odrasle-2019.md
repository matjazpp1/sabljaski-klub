  Last weekend the national fencing championships took place at Dolgi most. Our club had 28 entries in the individual events, winning a total of 15 medals: 5 gold, 3 silver and 7 bronze.

  On Saturday, only our coach Eva competed for us, fencing in [senior women's foil](https://www.engarde-service.com/files/dp2019a/dp2019c/ffs/index.php?page=clasfinal.htm) and finishing **3rd**.  
  On Sunday, a whole army of our fencers took part!  
  In [boys' épée U-10](https://www.engarde-service.com/files/dp2019a/dp2019c/mip/index.php?page=clasfinal.htm), Brin Babnik and Viktor Mramor competed. Brin finished **2nd** and Viktor 6th.  
  In [boys' épée U-12](https://www.engarde-service.com/files/dp2019a/dp2019c/mmb/index.php?page=clasfinal.htm), Jon Ponikvar, Vito Hvala, Maks Krivec Križ and Nejc Hudej competed. Jon finished **1st**, Maks and Vito shared **3rd place**, and Nejc was 6th.  
  In [girls' épée U-14](https://www.engarde-service.com/files/dp2019a/dp2019c/mfmin/index.php?page=clasfinal.htm), Devi Kuščer and Nika Korošec competed. Devi finished **3rd** and Nika 6th.  
  In [boys' épée U-14](https://www.engarde-service.com/files/dp2019a/dp2019c/mmmin/index.php?page=clasfinal.htm), as many as 8 of our fencers competed! Jakob Pavlin finished **1st**, Lars Ponikvar **2nd**, Leon S. Černjak **3rd**, Brin Božič 6th, Jernej Mustar 7th, Martin Mlajevec 8th, Jaša Osojnik 9th and Jon Babnik 10th.  
  In [senior women's épée](https://www.engarde-service.com/files/dp2019a/dp2019c/mfs/index.php?page=clasfinal.htm), Eva Jeza, Eva Figel, Gaja Kores and Neža Mravlje competed. Eva J. finished **1st**, Eva F. **3rd**, Gaja 5th and Neža 7th.  
  In [senior men's épée](https://www.engarde-service.com/files/dp2019a/dp2019c/mms/index.php?page=clasfinal.htm), Jure Jeza, Bernard Baler, Andraž Goršak and Dominik Šteblaj competed. Jure finished **1st**, Bernard 7th, Andraž 10th and Dominik 12th.  
  In [veterans' épée](https://www.engarde-service.com/files/dp2019a/dp2019c/mmv/index.php?page=clasfinal.htm), Mojca Grebenc, Myles Geiran and Dominik S. Černjak competed. Mojca finished **1st**, Myles **2nd** and Dominik **3rd!**

  [More photos on our Facebook page](https://www.facebook.com/pg/sabljanjeljubljana/photos/?tab=album&album_id=2819519471456306)

  The championships also included a [team event](https://www.engarde-service.com/files/dp2019a/dp2019c/mis/index.php?page=clasfinal.htm), though more for fun than for real: fencers of all ages could compete, and teams could be mixed. Four teams took part, three of them from our club. Everyone fenced excellently and, above all, had a great time!

Pretekli vikend je na Dolgem mostu potekalo državno prvenstvo v sabljanju. Nastopov našega kluba v posamičnem delu tekmovanja je bilo 28, skupaj pa so naši sabljači prisabljali 15 medalj: 5 zlatih, 3 srebrne in 7 bronastih.

V soboto je od naših nastopila le trenerka Eva, ki je sabljala v kategoriji [floret članice](https://www.engarde-service.com/files/dp2019a/dp2019c/ffs/index.php?page=clasfinal.htm) in zasedla **3. mesto**.

V nedeljo pa je nastopilo malo morje naših sabljačev!

V kategoriji [meč dečki U-10](https://www.engarde-service.com/files/dp2019a/dp2019c/mip/index.php?page=clasfinal.htm) sta nastopila Brin Babnik in Viktor Mramor. Brin je zasedel **2. mesto**, Viktor pa 6. mesto.

V kategoriji [meč dečki U-12](https://www.engarde-service.com/files/dp2019a/dp2019c/mmb/index.php?page=clasfinal.htm) so nastopili Jon Ponikvar, Vito Hvala, Maks Krivec Križ in Nejc Hudej. Jon je zasedel **1. mesto**, Maks in Vito sta si delila **3. mesto**, Nejc pa je bil 6.

V kategoriji [meč deklice U-14](https://www.engarde-service.com/files/dp2019a/dp2019c/mfmin/index.php?page=clasfinal.htm) sta nastopili Devi Kuščer in Nika Korošec. Devi je zasedla **3. mesto**, Nika pa 6. mesto.

V kategoriji [meč dečki U-14](https://www.engarde-service.com/files/dp2019a/dp2019c/mmmin/index.php?page=clasfinal.htm) je nastopilo kar 8 naših! Jakob Pavlin je zasedel **1. mesto**, Lars Ponikvar **2. mesto**, Leon S. Černjak **3. mesto**, Brin Božič 6. mesto, Jernej Mustar 7. mesto, Martin Mlajevec 8. mesto, Jaša Osojnik 9. mesto in Jon Babnik 10. mesto.

V kategoriji [meč članice](https://www.engarde-service.com/files/dp2019a/dp2019c/mfs/index.php?page=clasfinal.htm) so nastopile Eva Jeza, Eva Figel, Gaja Kores in Neža Mravlje. Eva J. je zasedla **1. mesto**, Eva F. **3. mesto**, Gaja 5. mesto in Neža 7. mesto.

V kategoriji [meč člani](https://www.engarde-service.com/files/dp2019a/dp2019c/mms/index.php?page=clasfinal.htm) so nastopili Jure Jeza, Bernard Baler, Andraž Goršak in Dominik Šteblaj. Jure je zasedel **1. mesto**, Bernard 7. mesto, Andraž 10. mesto in Dominik 12. mesto.

V kategoriji [meč veterani](https://www.engarde-service.com/files/dp2019a/dp2019c/mmv/index.php?page=clasfinal.htm) so nastopili Mojca Grebenc, Myles Geiran in Dominik S. Černjak. Mojca je zasedla **1. mesto**, Myles **2. mesto**, Dominik pa **3. mesto**!

[Več slik na našem Facebooku](https://www.facebook.com/pg/sabljanjeljubljana/photos/?tab=album&album_id=2819519471456306)

Na državnem prvenstvu je potekalo tudi [ekipno tekmovanje](https://www.engarde-service.com/files/dp2019a/dp2019c/mis/index.php?page=clasfinal.htm), vendar bolj za šalo kot za res; tekmovali so lahko sabljači vseh starosti, ekipe pa so lahko sestavljali predstavniki obeh spolov. Ekipnega dela tekmovanja so se udeležile štiri ekipe, od tega tri iz našega kluba. Vsi so sabljali odlično, predvsem pa so uživali!
