  ## Round 4 – 19 May 2019

  On Sunday, 19 May 2019, the last round of the 2018/2019 league took place. The league is over: Bernard Baler finished first among cadets and boys B, and Maks among boys C and D.

  This time our referee was Dominik, who holds the title of "trainee referee" after completing the referee seminar.

  ## Round 3 – 24 March 2019

  The third round of the 2018/2019 league took place on Sunday, 24 March 2019, at the Ljubljana Fencing Club, Tržaška 132, Ljubljana. In both categories the fencers fenced two rounds of pools, and these results gave us the new ranking.

## 4. krog – 19. 5. 2019

V nedeljo, 19. 5. 2019, je potekal še zadnji krog lige 2018/2019. Ligo smo zaključili, na prvem mestu sta Bernard Baler med kadeti in dečki B ter Maks med dečki C in D.

Tokrat nam je sodil Dominik, ki ima po opravljenem sodniškem seminarju naziv »sodnik pripravnik«.
